About the University

KIET (Deemed to be University) is located on the Meerut Road (NH-58) in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. Spread across a clean, green campus, the institution provides students with well-maintained classrooms, digital library resources, computer centers, and active innovation facilities.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs approved by regulatory authorities and is accredited with an NAAC 'A+' grade. Our emphasis remains on strong theoretical foundations balanced with practical laboratory sessions and continuous academic mentoring.

CSIT Department Spotlight

Department of Computer Science & Information Technology

B.Tech in Computer Science & Information Technology (CSIT)

The CSIT Department focuses on core computing principles alongside contemporary web and information systems. Students learn foundational algorithms, systems engineering, and practical software design to prepare for technology careers and academic pursuits.

Program Duration 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Sanctioned Intake 180 Seats
Focus Areas Web, Databases, Systems & Cloud

Department Contact: csit.office@kiet.edu | HOD Office: Academic Block A, 2nd Floor

Other Key Programs

B.Tech - Computer Science (CSE)

Covers computer algorithms, operating systems, compiler design, and software architecture.

B.Tech - CS (AI & ML)

Introduction to machine intelligence, statistical modeling, neural networks, and data analytics.

B.Tech - Information Technology

Focuses on enterprise networking, web servers, cybersecurity fundamentals, and databases.

MCA (Computer Applications)

Postgraduate degree covering advanced application development, systems design, and project work.
